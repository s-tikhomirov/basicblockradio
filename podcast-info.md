## Podcast Metadata

- Language: Russian with mixed technical terminology
- Format: Long-form interviews, 1.5 to 2 hours
- Focus: Technical blockchain topics, not financial speculation or price discussion
- Guests: Core developers, protocol founders, technical experts with hands-on experience
- Tagline: "Blockchain without bullshit"
- Approach: Friendly but rigorous, precise questions, deep technical dive
- Goal: Understand how things work
- Not adversarial: Do not roast guests or present them unfavorably
